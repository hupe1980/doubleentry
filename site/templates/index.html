Rust · accounting · cryptographic audit

Bookkeeping you can prove.

An immutable, tamper-evident double-entry bookkeeping engine. Entries are balanced by construction, money is exact integer arithmetic, and every closed period emits a seal a third party can verify — without being handed your books.

Get started API reference

cargo add doubleentry

What it guarantees

Balanced by construction

An entry reaches a persistable state only by passing validation, and the validated type has no other constructor. Unbalanced data cannot reach storage without a bug in the crate.

Exact

Money is a scaled integer with compile-time precision. No floating point, no wrapping, and every operation that can overflow returns a Result instead of panicking.

Deterministic

No clock, no random number generator, no hash-map iteration order. Identical inputs produce identical bytes, hashes and orderings — which is what makes replay and reproducible testing possible.

Verifiable

Entries are leaves in an append-only Merkle log. An auditor holding only a tree head can be given an O(log n) proof that an entry is included, and that the log was only ever appended to.

Gross-preserving

Balances carry debit and credit totals. An account showing zero may have seen no activity or heavy offsetting turnover, and a net alone cannot tell them apart.

Yours to shape

No chart of accounts, no reporting engine, no opinion about your domain. Accounts are paths in a hierarchy you define; reporting axes are named by you and never interpreted.

The question an auditor actually asks

Not “does this balance?” — most systems manage that — but “how do I know this record wasn't changed after the fact?”

Inclusion

This entry sits at this index under this root, in O(log n) hashes, revealing nothing else.

Consistency

The earlier log is a prefix of the later one. Not merely linked — provably append-only.

Balance

This account held this balance when the period closed, proven from the seal alone and named without disclosing the chart.
