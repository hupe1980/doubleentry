Bookkeeping you can prove.

An immutable, tamper-evident double-entry bookkeeping engine. One rule core decides every write, money is exact integer arithmetic, and every sealed period commits to balances a third party can verify without seeing your books.

Get started API reference

cargo add doubleentry

What it guarantees

Balanced at the write

One rule core decides every entry — over a database, inside the store's write transaction under the ledger's lock. Every entry balances per currency and layer, and audit() decides each one again. A payment and its assignment to open invoices commit as one batch.

Exact

Money is an i128 at a compile-time scale, each currency declared with its exponent. No floats, no operators, explicit rounding, and every overflow a Result.

Deterministic

An entry's id is derived from its idempotency key, so a retry replays instead of booking twice. The rule core reads no clock and no randomness.

Verifiable

Every record is a leaf of one RFC 6962 SHA-256 log. An auditor holding a tree head gets O(log n) proofs that an entry is included and that the log only grew.

Witnessable

The log head is a C2SP signed checkpoint that stock witnesses — somebody else's — cosign. The crate ships no witness and no HTTP client: requests and replies are bytes.

Audited by replay

audit() replays every record through the rule core and compares every table a store keeps, byte for byte. A superuser's edit is detected, not prevented.

Gross-preserving

Balances keep debit and credit totals, so a zero balance after heavy turnover is told apart from no activity.

Hierarchical

Accounts are a tree of paths and only leaves take postings. A rollup folds a trial balance up the tree and infers the grouping nodes.

Yours to shape

No chart of accounts and no opinion about your domain: paths, classes and reporting axes are yours. In memory, or over SQLite and PostgreSQL.

The question an auditor actually asks

Not “does this balance?” — most systems manage that — but “how do I know this record wasn't changed after the fact?”

Inclusion

This entry sits at this index under this root, in O(log n) hashes, revealing nothing else.

Consistency

The earlier log is a prefix of the later one. Not merely linked — provably append-only.

Balance

This account held this balance when the period closed, proven against a tree head and named — without disclosing any other account.

And the one no proof answers alone — “is this the history you showed everybody else?” — is answered by independent witnesses, who refuse to cosign a second history at a size they already vouched for.
